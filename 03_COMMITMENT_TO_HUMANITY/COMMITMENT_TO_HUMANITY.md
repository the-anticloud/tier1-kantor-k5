## Beyond Technology

`KANTOR_K5` exists within a broader project of sovereign AI — the belief that intelligence infrastructure should belong to humanity, not to a handful of cloud providers. This document articulates what Anticloud FZ LLE commits to, through `KANTOR_K5`, for human benefit.

## Human-Centered Design

**Interpretability First**
Every inference made by systems built on `KANTOR_K5` is logged with its full context, inputs, and outputs in the AIOSS chain. Humans — researchers, regulators, auditors — can always inspect what happened. No black box.

**Accessibility of Capability**
`KANTOR_K5` runs on commodity T4 GPUs at $0.08 per million tokens — a 750× cost reduction versus GPT-4. This is deliberate. High-capability AI should not be a luxury for well-funded organizations. A rural hospital, a public-sector agency, a university research group should be able to deploy sovereign AI.

**Non-Weaponisation**
`KANTOR_K5` is not licensed or designed for autonomous weapons systems, mass surveillance, or use against civilian populations. The Anticommons Enterprise License 1.0 includes explicit use restrictions aligned with international humanitarian law.

**Long-Term Thinking**
Anticloud's System of Things (SoT) architecture — which `KANTOR_K5` participates in — is designed around proprioceptive self-awareness, not nociceptive stimulus-response. The architecture is built to be auditable, correctable, and aligned across time horizons beyond a single inference.

## Commitments to Future Generations

The KANTOR K5 post-quantum hash scheme (USPTO pending) ensures that audit records created today remain verifiable even against quantum computing attacks decades from now. This project is building infrastructure that will outlast its creators.
